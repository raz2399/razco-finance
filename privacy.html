Razi-Nova Finance

Privacy Policy

Razi-Nova Finance · Razco Foods Supermarket · Last updated 29 October 2026

What this application is. Razi-Nova Finance is private back-office bookkeeping software built and operated by Razco Foods Supermarket for its own use. It is not a consumer product. It has no public sign-up, and the only people who hold accounts on it are the owner and authorized staff of Razco Foods. The bank account it connects to is the business’s own account.

Who we are

Razco Foods Supermarket, Lindsay, California, United States. Razi-Nova Finance is our internal system; we are the only operator of it and the only party it serves.

What information the system holds

  • Business banking information. Transactions, balances, account names and account numbers in masked form for the business bank accounts we connect ourselves. This is obtained through Plaid Inc. and is read-only — the system cannot move money, initiate payments or transfer funds through the bank connection.
  • Vendor and bookkeeping records. Supplier names, invoices, amounts, due dates, check numbers and payment history for the business.
  • Store sales figures. Daily totals by tender, department and register, read from our own point-of-sale system.
  • Staff account information. For each person authorized to use the system: an email address, a stored password in hashed form, and a record of sign-ins. Nothing more.

The system does not hold information about the store’s customers. It does not collect any personal information from members of the public, and no member of the public can create an account on it.

Why we hold it

To run the business: to know what the store owes its suppliers, to record payments, to reconcile the bank account against those records, and to keep the books required for accounting and tax purposes. There is no other purpose.

How the bank connection works

We connect our own bank accounts using Plaid, which handles the bank sign-in. We never see or store the bank username or password — those are entered into Plaid’s own interface, not ours. Plaid returns an access credential, which is held only on our server, encrypted at rest, and is never sent to a web browser or to the mobile application.

Plaid’s handling of information is governed by its own policy, which can be read at plaid.com/legal.

Who the information is shared with

We do not sell information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing of any kind. The only outside parties that handle it are the three service providers that run the system on our behalf:

  • Plaid Inc. — the bank connection.
  • Supabase — the database, hosted in the United States.
  • Netlify — the web hosting and the server code.

Beyond these, information is disclosed only to our accountant in the normal course of preparing the business’s accounts and tax filings, or where we are legally required to disclose it.

How it is protected

  • All connections to the application use TLS 1.2 or better. Nothing is transmitted unencrypted.
  • The database is encrypted at rest and is not reachable from the public internet.
  • Access requires a signed-in account. Every request to the server is checked against that account before any data is returned.
  • Bank credentials are never stored by us in any form. The Plaid access credential is held server-side only.

How long it is kept

Financial and bookkeeping records are kept for seven years, which is the period our records must cover for accounting and tax purposes. Bank transaction history is kept for the same period. A staff account and its sign-in history are deleted when that person no longer works for us.

Disconnecting the bank

We can end the bank connection at any time from within the application, which revokes the access credential with Plaid. Doing so stops any further transactions being retrieved.

Children

The application is a business tool and is not directed at or available to anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If the system changes in a way that affects what it holds or who it is shared with, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
